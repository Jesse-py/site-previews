Services

What we do

From the yearly sweep to a full rebuild, plus the uninvited guests that move into a warm flue.

Chimney cleaning

Annual cleaning removes the creosote that fuels chimney fires and clears the flue so smoke and carbon monoxide go up and out. A professional cleaning usually includes a basic inspection.

Chimney repair

  • Structural: masonry, crowns, rebuilds
  • Safety: relining, firebox repair, smoke chamber parging
  • Preventative: waterproofing, cap installation, flashing repair

Chimney caps installed

Heavy-duty stainless steel or copper caps, custom fitted. The mesh lets smoke out and keeps even the smallest critters out, the cap keeps rain and snow from eroding the mortar and rusting the damper, and the spark arrestor keeps embers off your roof.

Animal removal

Scratching, chirping or rustling behind the damper? We inspect, safely and humanely remove the animals and their nests, then clean, sanitize and deodorize. We clear:

  • Birds & chimney swifts
  • Raccoons & squirrels
  • Bats & small rodents

Dryer vent cleaning

Lint gets past the trap and builds up in the duct until the dryer overheats, a leading cause of home fires. We clean the whole length of the duct, not just the parts a vacuum reaches. It lowers your energy bill and extends the life of your dryer, too.

Safety evaluations

If your fireplace isn't drafting correctly, call us right away. We check for structural integrity, blockages and dangerous deposits, so your fireplace runs safely all winter.

Why sweep every year?

A clean chimney is a safe chimney

The NFPA recommends an annual chimney inspection. Here's what a yearly sweep protects you from.

1

Creosote fires

Burning wood leaves creosote, a sticky, oily, highly flammable coating inside the chimney. If it builds up and gets too hot, it can start a chimney fire that spreads to the rest of the house in minutes. Annual cleaning removes the fuel.

2

Carbon monoxide

When soot, bird nests or debris clog the flue, odorless, invisible CO "back-puffs" into your living room instead of going up the flue. A clear flue keeps a proper draft.

3

Better heat

When air flows freely, the fire gets the oxygen it needs to burn hotter and more completely. You use less wood for the same heat.

4

Damage you can't see

The inspection catches cracked flue tiles, which can let heat reach your home's wooden frame, and damaged caps that let rain and snow in, leading to mold and expensive masonry rot.

Gas fireplaces need checks too

Gas burns much cleaner than wood, but spider webs or small debris can still clog the pilot or burner. That's often why a gas fireplace takes a few tries to stay lit.

Signs your dryer vent is clogged

Clothes take more than one cycle to dry, or the top of the dryer feels hot to the touch.

Safety tips

Keep your home safe this winter

Inspect every year

The NFPA recommends an annual chimney inspection before the burning season. It prevents fires and keeps your fireplace working at its best.

Know your creosote

Creosote is the primary cause of chimney fires. Professional removal protects your flue and gives you peace of mind every time you light a fire.

Burn seasoned wood

Use wood dried for at least six months. Green wood smokes and builds creosote faster. Seasoned wood is dark, cracked at the ends, and sounds hollow when knocked together.

CO detectors on every level

Along with regular sweeping, install carbon monoxide detectors on every level of your home.
