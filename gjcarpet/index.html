About us

A small mom and pop shop

GJ Carpet Cleaning is a family owned business operated by a father and son team. We have taken care of the floor cleaning needs of our extended family's rental business for years. In February 2020 we purchased a new truck mount steam cleaning machine to focus on providing a service to the general public, just like what we would want for our own homes.

We pride ourselves on being natives of the Grand Valley. Fourth and fifth generations to be exact. We have spent our lives training and honing our skills to be able to provide the best cleaning experience for our customers. We are a small mom and pop shop not controlled by a franchise. We are able to treat people how we want to be treated. We utilize a "whatever it takes" approach to all our tasks.

What makes us unique: our approach to quality and satisfaction. We take the time to do it right.
