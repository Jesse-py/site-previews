Services & prices

Every price, by your dog's size

Prices start at the amounts shown and depend on your dog's weight and coat. Dogs over 28 lbs, or with long or difficult coats, are assessed on an individual basis.

Full Groom

Bath, blow-out, brush-out, full hair cut, nail & pad trim.

Also includes ear cleaning & plucking, teeth brushing and anal gland expression: ask for them at each visit.

Full Groom, short-haired dogs

Bath, blow-out, brush-out, de-shed, nail & pad trim. For dogs like Bulldogs, Pugs, Chihuahuas, Boxers and Dalmatians.

Also includes ear cleaning & plucking, teeth brushing and anal gland expression: ask for them at each visit.

Bath Only

Bath, blow-out, brush-out and nail trim.

Also includes a face, feet & sani tidy up: ask for it at each visit.

Before you book

Three things to know

Vaccinations first

Every dog must be up to date on Rabies, Distemper and Bordetella. Bring the records in or email them to clipanddip33@gmail.com before you make an appointment.

Allow 3 hours

Please allow 3 hours for a full groom or bath. You'll get a call or text when your dog is ready to be collected.

Ask for the extras

Ear cleaning, teeth brushing, anal glands and the face, feet & sani tidy up are done when you ask for them at each visit, not automatically.
