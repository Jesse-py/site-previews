Hauling & junk removal

Priced by the trailer load

Brush and junk removal, and house, apartment and garage clean outs, for homes and businesses. One trailer is 14' x 7' x 4', about 14 cubic yards. You pay for the share of it your load fills.

  • 1/4 trailer$150+ tax
  • 1/2 trailer$250+ tax
  • 3/4 trailer$300+ tax
  • Full trailer$400+ tax
  • Minimum per trip: $100.
  • First hour of loading is included in the full trailer price; $40 per hour after the first hour.
  • Prices vary depending on the materials and ease of access to the materials.
  • Additional fees may exist for some items (tires, appliances, couches, mattresses) and for weights over 1 ton.
  • Vehicle hauling is priced per mile. Mulch, rock, gravel and sand are priced by weight and distance.

Our team

Meet Don and Shawna

We are Don and Shawna, a husband and wife team that recently welcomed our beautiful daughter into the world. We're a family business in the beautiful East Mountains of New Mexico, here to offer a helping hand with the services listed, and we're open to other services too. Please feel free to ask us if a service is available.

We both worked as first responders in New Mexico for many years. We are very passionate about helping and serving those around us. We are not afraid to work hard and get our hands dirty.

When we're not working we enjoy our family time: in our garden, or hanging out with our dogs and chickens on our little homestead.

Why "Scout's"? Our namesake and logo are inspired by our bluetick hound, Scout! He is full of energy and loves to play ball.
