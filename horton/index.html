Tips from the shop

Get your snowblower ready for winter

A properly tuned snowblower makes all the difference when the first snowfall hits.

  1. Check fuel and spark plugOld fuel can gum up your engine. Drain last season's gas or use fresh fuel with stabilizer. A clean, properly gapped plug starts reliably.
  2. Inspect belts and augerCheck belts for cracks or fraying and make sure the auger spins freely.
  3. Lubricate moving partsGrease the chute, auger bearings and other moving parts to prevent rust.
  4. Test start and runRun it before the first snowfall to catch issues early.
  5. Professional tune-upSometimes it's easier and safer to let a pro handle the prep.

Snowblower tune-ups, repairs and maintenance, with $25 pick-up and delivery in Mansfield.

5 signs your mower needs a tune-up

Many problems are easy to spot early, before they turn into costly repairs.

  1. Hard starting or won't startOften a dirty spark plug, stale fuel or carburetor issues.
  2. Rough idling or stallingShaking, sputtering or stalling usually means the carburetor, air filter or worn parts need attention.
  3. Uneven cuttingDull blades or a misaligned deck leave the lawn patchy.
  4. Excessive smoke or oil leaksOften worn gaskets or a needed oil change.
  5. Unusual noisesGrinding, squealing or rattling can mean loose belts, worn bearings or engine trouble.

Regular tune-ups every season save time and money and extend the life of your mower.
