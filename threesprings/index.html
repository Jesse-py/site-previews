Our wood

Local hardwoods, processed on site

We cut and acquire local hardwoods, and all of our wood is processed on site. Firewood is cut at 16-18 inches and is a mix of local hardwoods, primarily:

  • Red oak
  • White oak
  • Locust
  • Ash
  • Hard maple
  • Hickory
About seasoningThis time of year we can provide wood seasoned for 1-2 years, but orders are processed on demand, so in fall and winter please allow about a week for it to air dry before burning. We don't have a kiln yet, so we don't promise kiln-dried moisture levels. USDA guidelines call wood "dry" at 25% moisture and below; best is under 20%.

Burn wisely

Get more heat from every cord

  1. Burn dry, seasoned wood, at least 6-12 months old. It starts easier, burns hotter, and you use less of it.
  2. Split, stack, cover and store your wood. Split wood dries much faster than whole logs.
  3. Start with all-natural fire-starters and small, dry kindling. They warm the flue so it starts drafting sooner.
  4. Leave space between pieces so air can flow, and add small pieces once the fire is burning well.
  5. Follow your stove manufacturer's instructions, and don't leave the room until the stove door is closed.
