About us

Family-owned, with nearly two decades in the business

BW Welding & Fabrication LLC opened its doors in May 2020, but owner Bobby Wilson brings over 18 years of welding experience to every project. With 14 straight years specializing in aluminum boat welding, Bobby has honed his skills and expertise to deliver top-notch quality and craftsmanship on every job.

Bobby is also a WABO certified structural steel welder and AWS certified aluminum welder, so you can trust that all welding is done to strict industry standards. Attention to detail and personal oversight on every project are cornerstones of our business.
