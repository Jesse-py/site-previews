Cabinet refinishing

How we paint cabinets, the right way

Here's exactly what happens to your cabinets, step by step.

  1. Take apart and clean

    Doors and drawers come off, then everything is cleaned and de-greased.

  2. Sand

    Doors are sanded with 180-grit to remove the old finish (not to bare wood), using a cabinet sander with a HEPA filter to contain the dust.

  3. Prime

    An oil or urethane cabinet primer from Benjamin Moore, machine-sprayed, then the pieces cure on drying racks.

  4. Sand again

    A fine 320-grit pass for a smooth base.

  5. Top coats

    3-4 coats of urethane cabinet enamel, sanded between coats. Final coat Benjamin Moore Cabinet Command or Fine Paints of Europe (an optional upgrade). High gloss is available for extra durability.

  6. Cure and care

    After 15-30 days of curing, clean with a microfiber towel and mild dish soap (an oil-based furniture soap for clear-coated cabinets).

Questions

Ask the painter

Are estimates free?

Yes. We come out for a free on-site estimate and consultation, scheduled around your availability. Call or text (845) 742-3237, email us, or send the form below.

How much does exterior or interior painting cost?

It depends on how much prep and repair the job needs and the amount of materials, which comes from the square footage and how many coats it takes for full coverage. That's why every estimate is done on site.

How much does cabinet painting cost?

It's based on the number of openings (doors, drawers, shelves and other pieces to paint), the finish you'd like, and how much prep it takes to remove the old coating. Most unpainted cabinets just need a careful scuff sanding first.

Does cedar siding need oil primer?

In most cases, yes. Cedar has rich tannins and oils that can bleed through a top coat and shorten its life. Red oak and other hardwoods may also need specific or oil primers to bond if they've never been painted.

Can you paint vinyl siding?

In most cases, no. Vinyl can expand and contract as much as an inch in sun and heat, and no paint holds up to that intact. Wash vinyl siding with detergent once a year to keep it looking good.

Can exterior painting be done in cold weather?

Yes and no. Low-temperature paints make it possible, but below about 35 degrees the paint won't bond or cure properly overnight or between coats.

Is cabinet paint different from wall paint?

Yes. It's made to stick to furniture and millwork and to be more durable. Modern cabinet paints are water based, but they're 1K or 2K urethanes, not ordinary latex.
