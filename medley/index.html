5 signs your septic tank needs pumped

If you notice any of these, call us for a free quote.

  1. It's been longer than 3-5 years.

    As a general rule, your septic tank will need to be pumped every 3-5 years. This may vary based on your household size, usage and tank size.

  2. Your toilet clogs.

    If it's only one toilet, it could just be a simple clog. But if several toilets are clogging or are slow to flush, it may be time to have your tank pumped.

  3. There's a foul smell.

    A "healthy" septic tank is designed to work without producing any odor. If you notice a "sewer" smell, you may need your tank pumped.

  4. There's standing water.

    The water around your septic tank may not be draining properly if the tank is full. Your tank might also be overflowing.

  5. You hear gurgling, or the drains are slow to clear.

    As with the toilets, one slow drain may just be a clog. If several drains are clogged or slow to clear, it may be a full septic tank.
