FAQ

Questions people ask

Snow removal

Do you offer seasonal contracts?

Yes. Both per-visit pricing and full-season packages (from $350).

Will you clear the back of the house or decks?

The focus is usually driveways and front walkways, but if you need more cleared, just mention it in your request.

What time do you come?

Typically early morning, between 5am and 8am, depending on when the snow falls.

Pressure washing

Can you get rid of oil stains?

Mostly. Degreasers and scrubbing lift most stains before pressure washing. Complete removal is the goal, but it can't be promised for every stain.

Will this damage my siding or concrete?

No. Pressure and nozzles are adjusted to each surface to protect your property.

Do you clean fences or retaining walls?

Yes. Just ask in your quote request.

Driveway & paver sealing

Can you seal after I've already cleaned it?

Yes, as long as it's fully dry and not damaged.

How long does the sealer last?

Usually 2–3 years, depending on traffic and exposure.

Will it make my surface shiny?

Your choice: a natural/matte finish or an enhanced/glossy one.

Christmas lights

Do I own the lights?

No. You rent them for the season, so there's no storing, no replacing bulbs and no extra hassle.

What's included in the rental?

The lights, clips, timer, extension cords, installation, takedown and full maintenance for the season, all in one price.

What if a bulb goes out or something stops working?

Zane comes and fixes it, fast. That's part of the rental package.

Can I choose colors or styles?

Yes: classic warm white, multi-color, or a custom pattern (subject to availability).

Will you install lights I already own?

No. Store-bought lights often fail, don't fit properly and aren't built for clean installs. Prairie Pros lights are cut to fit your home, clip securely to the roofline, and come with full-season maintenance and takedown.

Fleet washing

Can you come to my yard or lot?

Yes, with all the equipment, and water too if needed (bringing water adds to the cost).

Do you offer regular service?

Yes: weekly, bi-weekly or a custom schedule.

Do you need power or water hookups?

No. The rig is self-contained if needed.
