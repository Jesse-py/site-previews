Appointments

Getting the best results

Good shoeing takes the farrier and the client working together. Here is what helps.

  • All clients are put on a six-week schedule, unless I set a different one for your horse.
  • Have your horse ready, available, reasonably clean, dry and well mannered by the appointment time.
  • Don't apply hoof dressings just before I arrive, and if it's feeding time, please don't feed other horses while I work.
  • The horse will stand better if it isn't tied up for a long time. If it's very energetic, lunge it before the appointment.
  • I may ask to see your horse walk or trot, to evaluate its way of going and check for lameness.
  • I'll let you know if I'm running more than 15 minutes late. Please let me know if you'll be late or can't make it.

Good to know

I work with every horse patiently. If the work can't be finished because of the horse's behavior, you'll need a veterinarian to tranquilize the horse, and the appointment may move to another day.

I don't refit the work of other farriers.

Ask me anything about the trimming and shoeing: during the appointment, or by email or phone.

Lost a shoe?

I'll replace a lost shoe (can't be found) or a cast shoe (found) as soon as possible. Except for foundered or unruly horses, a lost or cast shoe isn't an emergency, but please tell me at the earliest opportunity.
