Dustless hardwood floor refinishing

Bring those floors back to life

Maybe you found a once beautiful hardwood floor under old carpet, or you're moving into a home whose floors have seen better days. No need to replace good hardwood just because of dents, scratches, chips or discoloring.

Our dustless system uses powerful vacuums, trailer mounted or stand-alone units outside your home, connected to all of our sanding equipment. They capture the dust at the sander before it gets into the air.

Book a free assessment

The process

  1. Pre-sanding

    If there's carpet over your hardwood, we remove it and dispose of all related materials.

  2. Repairs

    Damaged boards are replaced with undamaged, reclaimed or new wood before any sanding.

  3. Dustless sanding

    Coarse grit up to fine, removing as little wood as possible. Dust, finish, dirt and wax are contained at the source.

  4. Buffing

    An abrasive screen blends the fine sanding marks until they're invisible to the eye.

  5. Staining

    We keep every Minwax color and lay test areas on your floor, so you see the real result, not a little chart.

  6. Finishing

    3 coats of oil-based or 4 coats of water-based polyurethane over several days, buffed between coats. Keep rugs off 2-4 weeks while it cures.
